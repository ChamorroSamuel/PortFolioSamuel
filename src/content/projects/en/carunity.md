## What it is

CarUnity is a web and mobile platform for car enthusiasts. Users can list their vehicles, explore car auctions and connect with a passionate community, all in one place.

## Key features

- List and showcase your vehicles.
- Join and take part in car auctions.
- Connect with other car enthusiasts and grow your network.
- Secure bidding and transactions.
