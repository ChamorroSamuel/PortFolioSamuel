## What it is

Elderly Care is a health monitoring solution that helps doctors track the vital signs of older patients in real time. It integrates with smartwatches to provide continuous insights and emergency alerts.

## Key features

- Real-time health monitoring through smartwatch integration.
- Emergency alerts for doctors and caregivers.
- Medication reminders and health recommendations.
- Secure, simple access to patient records.
