## What it is

DittoBox is a cloud storage platform that keeps your important files secure and always available. It organises information intelligently and protects it with advanced encryption.

## Key features

- Storage with end-to-end encryption.
- Tools to share files and collaborate.
- Automatic backups and version control.
- Sync across devices so you can reach your files anywhere.
