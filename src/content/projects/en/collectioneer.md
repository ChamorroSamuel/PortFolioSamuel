## What it is

Collectioneer is an app built for collectors. You can showcase your collectibles, engage with a community of enthusiasts and take part in auctions, all within a smooth, easy-to-use platform.

## Key features

- Upload and display your collectible items.
- Connect with other collectors and grow your network.
- Join auctions or host your own inside the app.
- Secure bidding and transactions.
